03/The craft

Seedance 2.5 in Zetica

The prompt is the craft.

Tyler's videos are generated with Seedance 2.5 in Zetica, and the magic is in the writing. His prompts run long and read like shot lists, because the model gives back exactly as much direction as you put in. Learn the writing and the same studio answers to you.

  1. 01

    Long prompts, on purpose

    These aren't one-liners. A finished prompt reads like a shot list: subject, wardrobe, lighting, lens, camera move. Every beat is written out so the model has nothing left to guess.

  2. 02

    Seedance 2.5 takes direction

    All that writing pays off: @ tags for style, precise camera moves, multi-shot sequences that keep characters consistent, and dialogue, music, and effects rendered in sync with the picture.

  3. 03

    Rewrite, re-roll, refine

    Generations land in a working library. Tweak a line, re-run the shot, keep what works. Taste is a loop, not a lottery.

Everything above runs in the same Zetica anyone can open: same Seedance 2.5, same tools, no private build. The writing is a skill, and you can start learning it today.
